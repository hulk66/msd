Font licensing notes

- Original site typeface: **Invention** (variable, with italics variant), served
  from the msd.com WordPress theme. Invention is a proprietary commercial
  typeface; its license could not be verified to permit self-hosting by a
  third party, so it is NOT shipped in this repo.
- Substitute in use: **Inter** (variable, weights 300/400/700), SIL Open Font
  License 1.1 — https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt
  File: inter-variable.woff2 (downloaded from Google Fonts, static snapshot).
- Substitution must be confirmed or replaced by the Merck brand team before
  launch (see inventory/block-library.md and the spec's risks table).
- roboto-*.woff2 files ship with the aem-boilerplate scaffold and are unused
  by our styles; candidates for removal at final review.
